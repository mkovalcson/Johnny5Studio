# Johnny 5 Laser Range

The range now includes enemy fire, three hits per life and three lives, disassembly/reassembly respawning, optional narrated instruction-sequence playback, configurable event audio, and designated observation-platform teleporting.

Start SteamVR > tap A > Game · Johnny 5 Laser Range > Start game.

Left stick: drive/turn Johnny. Right stick Y: aim the laser. Trigger: fire (or skip instructions). Tap A: menu. Hold A: aim a gravity arc with the right controller/right stick, then release over a green lookout to teleport. Only designated platform landings are accepted.

Choose media in Game setup · Sequence and audio (VR) or Laser Range setup… (desktop). No sequence is selected by default, so the game starts ready on the first lookout. Audio has bundled defaults. Choices are stored in Config/VR/LaserRange.json. Exiting restores the normal backdrop and your previous viewpoint.

See **VR-Laser-Range-Combat-And-Teleport.md** for the full controls, startup flow, lives, media paths and validation details.
