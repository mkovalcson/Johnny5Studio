# VR rehearsal panel

In the VR Editor, open **Playback → Compact transport panel**. You can also start Record or Overdub from the body recording menu, which brings up the rehearsal panel automatically. The panel can be moved by its top bar while idle.

1. Choose **Record** or **Overdub** from the current timeline cursor. For Overdub, the cursor must be before the end of the sequence. Capture uses the armed groups from **Choose recording groups**; the three tracked head axes remain off by default.
2. Release the trigger and stand upright with arms relaxed. Once tracking is ready, the panel counts down **3, 2, 1**. Cancel or press Right B to abort before it starts.
3. The panel shows the recording duration. Select **Stop** or press Right B to review the draft.
4. **Replay** plays the draft at 1× with existing sequence audio and unarmed motion. It stops at the end of the take, or use the panel's Stop. The original cursor and playback rate return after replay. The draft stays available for repeated review.
5. **Keep** adds the take as one undoable timeline edit; save the sequence afterward. **Discard** clears the draft without writing commands.

Replay uses temporary playback data and does not drive physical hardware. The scene bridge update requires the matching VR Player and VR Editor from the same package. Existing Config, calibration and sequence files are not replaced.
