# Calibration alignment help — 2026-11-09

All 21 guided calibration steps now show live readiness before and during capture. The target-motion drawing remains distinct for wrist bending, wrist rotation and upper-arm twist. A second figure shows your measured hand positions, estimated shoulder/elbow joints and reference zones. Green means the measured requirement is satisfied; amber identifies the side or body part that needs correction. Dashed joints are estimates from the headset/controllers, not additional body trackers. Forward/back corrections are written in text because the live figure is a front view.

The checklist shows actual measurements and acceptance limits: hand heights, shoulder-fit reach, bent-arm reach, neutral forearm position, wrist displacement and movement angles, opposite endpoints, separation of bend and roll axes, arm-twist direction, available finger sensors, open/closed finger range, crouch depth and forward-bend distance. The first blocking requirement also appears prominently above the figures. Where possible, corrections say raise/lower, move left/right or forward/back and show centimetres. The capture validator is also consulted so that an acceptance failure is not hidden behind a green readiness indicator.

Pull and release Trigger once to arm a step. Correct amber items and hold still; capture proceeds automatically when aligned. Capturing requires 1.2 seconds of steady input, at least 20 samples, hand position variation no greater than 2.5 cm and rotation variation no greater than approximately 11.5 degrees. A reset shows its reason. Finger steps retain the three-second preparation countdown after releasing the trigger. Tracking loss, paused input and unavailable finger data are identified explicitly; unavailable fingers remain optional so arm/body calibration can finish without them.

Wrist checks now allow for estimated controller travel around the wrist pivot rather than treating every translation over 8 cm as arm movement. The allowance grows with rotation, up to 16 cm; this is a grip-offset estimate. Each endpoint must move at least 10 degrees from neutral. The opposite endpoint must be on the other side, and roll must differ sufficiently from bending. These checks cannot directly verify elbow placement or prove that a user rotated only one anatomical joint with three tracked devices.

Install: close VR Player and VR Editor and extract J5Studio-VR-Calibration-Alignment-Help-update.zip over the existing portable VR folder, preserving its subfolders. This is cumulative since Johnny5VR-0.9.0-cumulative-update-2026-11-08.zip and includes the subsequent independent wrist calibration and repeated-work performance changes. Config, Settings, models and saved calibration are not included or overwritten. The regular Desktop Editor is not deployed. Existing profiles remain usable; run guided calibration to measure new wrist/arm ranges when needed.

Validation includes the complete 21-step sequence, readiness and tracking loss at every step, wrist displacement/angle/opposite-endpoint rejection, automatic waiting/resuming after correcting alignment, optional finger data, profile round trips and the existing Follow/recording checks. UI previews are rendered by the same drawing routine used in VR. Physical controller calibration still needs testing on the gaming PC.
