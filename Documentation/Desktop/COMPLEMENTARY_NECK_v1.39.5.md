# Complementary neck nod and tilt — 1.39.5

NeckNodUp and NeckTiltRight are no longer exclusive "owners" of the shared neck pistons. Both apply at the same time and always resolve into the two pistons in one place, `NeckMix`: per piston `u = s(Nod)·nod + s(Tilt)·tilt`, clamped to ±100 of that piston's own travel, where `s` is −1 when ServoConfig reverses the piston for that parent. Clamping each piston independently keeps nod's full range and gives tilt whatever travel is left: with full nod, a tilt can only move one piston. Pistons are carried in NeckNodUp child units, so the existing pulse mapping (gang direction and servo Reverse) is unchanged; nod-only and tilt-only produce exactly the previous pulses.

Applied everywhere:

- Hardware (`HardwareManager`): a Nod or Tilt command updates the shared state and re-drives both pistons; a direct piston command replaces that piston and re-derives nod/tilt so the next command continues smoothly.
- URDF view: pistons are always held in Nod units; the pose decomposes them into effective nod (drawn with the Nod calibration) and tilt (Tilt calibration). Identical piston pulses now give an identical pose whichever parent they came through (previously 7.5° vs 6.0°). Calibrated-motion frames only update the display, so they no longer drop a component.
- Timeline: the merged "shared neck" curve with owners is gone. Nod and Tilt are ordinary rows with their own spline checkboxes, curves, breaks and export samples. Library poses save both values; Movie carry-over no longer tracks an owner.
- Exports resolve the neck into piston commands in both modes (gang-adjusted values for Individual, matching expanded ganged commands).
- Face tracking sends headPitch to Nod and headRoll to Tilt; the mixer resolves them (it previously scaled both down together at the limits, sacrificing nod). LPiston/RPiston still work as direct piston targets.
- Controller recording, recording replay, the command editor's spline draft and Break Spline treat Nod and Tilt independently. The Headroom editor's neck motion starts from the mixed pistons.
- Help: Spline Editor › Neck nod and tilt; Commands, Controls/Hotkeys, Movie Timeline and Webcam Face Tracking updated. The spline editor's shared-point owner toggles no longer apply.

Validation: Release build 1.39.5 succeeded with the existing CS0649 warnings. New `--neck` suite: 70 assertions (pulse equivalence for nod-only and tilt-only at every 10 units, clipping at full nod up and down, round-trips, direct pistons under either parent, continuity after a direct piston, export resolution, and URDF poses for combined, clipped, reordered and child-commanded inputs). Two existing assertions that encoded the shared curve were updated (Program spline draft, SplineWorkflow breaks) and the Break Spline curve check rewritten. All 36 suites were compared with the pre-change build: 33 identical (including 13 that stop at the same pre-existing first assertion on this machine), general/break-spline now pass, and `--neck` is new. Because those 13 suites stop early, calibrated-speed motion, the collision safeguard and controller-speed paths are covered only by `--neck` and a manual check. Physical robot output needs a low-speed manual test.
