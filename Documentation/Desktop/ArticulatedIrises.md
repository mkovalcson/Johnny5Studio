# Twenty-leaf irises and clear eye covers

Installed November 3, 2026 in both managed robot models:

- `Config/Models/Robots/Johnny5Head/johnny5_head.urdf`
- `Config/Models/Robots/Johnny5FullBody/johnny5_head.urdf`

Both eyes retain the original `left_iris_disc` / `right_iris_disc` visual names,
`LeftIris` / `RightIris` joints, `Iris Close` parent control, and independent child
controls. The existing calibration, reversal, opening conversion and 0.1 mm
geometry update threshold are unchanged. The existing conversion maps 100%,
55%, and 10% aperture to 1.80, 0.90, and 0.30 inch openings respectively.
Saved and embedded calibration can restrict those extents and is preserved.

The new `Meshes/EyeMechanism/iris_twenty_blades.stl` supplies the neutral appearance
to ordinary URDF readers. The editor regenerates the twenty visible leaf surfaces
as the existing iris control changes, sweeping the overlapping seam pattern.
This is a rendering representation, not a validated rigid mechanical linkage or
a manufacturing drawing. Twenty is the approved mockup count; no authoritative
blade count was found for the Wollensak 209 mm f/4.5 Copy lens.

The initial 0.012 mm seams were barely visible in the real renderer. Build 2.9.5
increases them to 0.10 mm and deepens the shallow leaf shading to 0.12 mm, matching
the visible overlap pattern in the approved mockup more closely. The aperture
diameter, number of leaves and control mapping are unchanged. The initial mesh
is backed up alongside each original URDF as
`iris_twenty_blades-before-contrast.stl`. `--refresh-iris-meshes` regenerates only
the two neutral blade mesh files and reruns the iris checks.

The new `iris_clear_lens.stl` covers exactly the iris's 45.72 mm outer diameter.
Each cover follows its eye's existing hierarchy. Its edge is at local X=3.5 mm
and its convex surface rises another 6 mm (reduced from the 8 mm trial dome). The editor supplies smooth
surface normals and a low-opacity, high-gloss material. WPF and DX11 render
transparency and specular highlights. Build 2.9.9 reflects the robot's existing
scene lights; it does not refract or sample the actual backdrop/video. Clear covers add no collision proxies. Original collision
geometry, fasteners, other visuals, and joint definitions are preserved.

Build 2.9.6 makes the clear cover easier to see: the center remains nearly
transparent, while a radial edge-opacity gradient outlines the perimeter and
a broader, stronger specular response catches scene lighting. Explicit UVs
carry the same gradient into the DX11 transparent texture pass. This treatment
affects only `iris_clear_lens`; the pupil's black/RGB behavior is unchanged.

Build 2.9.7 replaces that subtle tint with world-space studio softbox reflections
on the convex cover. Fresnel response strengthens reflections at grazing angles.
The reflection texture updates with the camera and animated eye/head/presentation
pose in the existing synchronization phase, before DX11 scene upload. The same
texture is used by WPF, DX11 and snapshots. A cached view signature skips stationary
updates; precomputed optical normals and a reusable pixel buffer reduce moving-view
cost. No additional timer or video/backdrop readback is introduced.

Build 2.9.8 replaces the original shallow parabolic cover with a spherical cap
having 5 mm sag at the same 22.86 mm radius. Surface normals used by the mesh
and environment reflection now share the spherical definition. Both model lens
STLs are regenerated by `--refresh-iris-lenses`; their previous shallow versions
are preserved as `iris_clear_lens-before-dome.stl` alongside the original URDF
backups. The edge position, aperture diameter, calibration and collision proxies
are unchanged.

Build 2.9.9 removes the virtual studio panels. Lens highlights now sample the
same live directional and point lights captured for the robot's WPF/DX11 lighting
profiles. Their directions/positions, colors, range and distance attenuation
drive soft, rounded specular glints. Ambient light only contributes to the
perimeter tint. Lighting changes invalidate the reflection cache even with a
stationary camera. Turning all robot lights off leaves no artificial reflection.
Glints move toward the lens edge or disappear as the viewing/light angles change.
The checks cover light movement, color, attenuation, range, disabling lights,
live URDF lighting-profile changes and actual WPF/DX11 close-ups.

Build 2.9.10 corrects the flatter appearance after the lighting change. The
5 mm dome geometry was unchanged in both URDF mesh assets and the runtime mesh.
Existing ambient scene radiance now contributes a curvature-dependent Fresnel
sheen over the glass, strongest at grazing angles. A glossier direct-light lobe
also distinguishes lens highlights from broad diffuse shading. This preserves
the body's real lighting sources and introduces no virtual studio rectangles.
Checks verify stronger grazing-angle sheen, a clear center, the unchanged 5 mm
dome, and fully disappearing reflections when all real lights are disabled.

The iris checks now verify reflected and clear pixels over the black pupil in
front and oblique WPF and actual GTX 1080 Ti DX11 captures, as well as camera
response and stationary caching. Hardware close-ups are in
`iris-previews/Dx11-front-lens.png` and `iris-previews/Dx11-side-lens.png`.

Only models containing the matching `_iris_clear_lens` visual opt into the new
blade rendering. Older or custom models retain their annular iris behavior.
The original URDFs are preserved under
`change-backups/articulated-irises-20261103/`.

Build the editor and checks in Release, then run `ServoAnimator.Checks --iris`
from the ServoAnimator workspace. Checks verify both models, all twenty leaves,
opening sizes, control routing, preserved geometry, clear cover dimensions,
WPF previews and DX11 material conversion. `--install-iris` performs the initial
model asset installation and preserves the originals; existing backups are never
overwritten. Preview PNGs are written to `iris-previews/`.

URDF Configuration now exposes Lens reflectivity in each renderer appearance profile.
`UrdfRenderProfile.LensReflectivity` defaults to 1 and ranges from 0 to 2. Missing
values in older configuration files retain the previous appearance. It scales the
generated reflection texture alpha after the original 65% cap and controls the
native lens specular brush as well, so zero suppresses both reflection sources in
WPF and DX11. The texture cache includes reflectivity; applying a configuration
refreshes the lens immediately without requiring camera motion. Save Default,
Back, profile cloning and renderer reset use the existing profile lifecycle.

The gold eye bezels now slope inward: their outer radius (30.7467 mm) and
outer-edge positions are unchanged, while the inner radius (24.796 mm) moves
1.155 mm rearward. The inner black bands move 0.125 mm forward, placing their
fronts 0.20 mm above the recessed gold inner edges. They retain a small seating
overlap with the gold and clear the unchanged lens rim. Both visual and collision
STLs are updated identically in both managed robot models; triangle normals are
recomputed after deformation. Iris controls, lens geometry and URDF definitions
are unchanged. Originals and measurements are archived under
`change-backups/sloped-eye-bezels-20261103/`. The installer is
`tools/slope_eye_bezels.py`; `tools/verify_eye_bezels.py` checks the fixed outer
edges, unchanged radii, exact offsets, normal directions and seating clearance.

The subsequent outer black lip adjustment raises only the outer radial half of
each black band by another 0.40 mm. Its radial split is 23.828 mm: the inner half
stays at X=3.40 mm and the outer half reaches X=3.80 mm, joined by a short step.
The back seating surface stays at X=3.18 mm. Gold bezels and lenses are unchanged.
`tools/raise_black_eye_lip.py` rebuilds closed visual/collision bands with checked
winding and unit normals in both models. Immediately preceding black meshes are
preserved in `change-backups/raised-black-eye-lip-20261103/`. The earlier flat-band
checks in `verify_eye_bezels.py` describe the previous state; this adjustment's
installer verifies the new profile, watertightness and matching model copies.

The gold-only follow-up recesses its inner edge another 0.50 mm (1.655 mm
total relative to the original gold mesh). The gold inner front is now X=2.70 mm;
all outer-edge vertices stay fixed. Black bands, lenses and iris blades retain
their previous geometry. Visual and collision meshes match the new slope in
both models. `tools/recess_gold_bezel_half_mm.py` installs and verifies this
change, with immediate originals and hash records preserved in
`change-backups/gold-bezel-extra-half-mm-20261103/`.

The recessed gold subsequently exposed the silver parent-eye backing. Its front
annulus is now pulled rearward beneath the gold, fading to zero displacement at
the original outer silver lip and at CAD Z=8.89 mm behind the front cap. A common
radial displacement across the cap preserves its thickness. The affected visual
is `eye_lens_cad1ee_without_gimbal_screws.stl`; the corresponding full collision
mesh receives the same correction in both models. Other eye meshes and URDFs
remain byte-identical. `tools/recess_silver_eye_backing.py` verifies the local
change, unchanged outer lip, retained triangle topology, normals and hashes.
Original meshes, hashes and before previews are archived under
`change-backups/recess-silver-eye-backing-20261103/`. Eye previews in `--iris`
now include the silver backing as well as the iris, bezel and glass, so this
interaction is visible during verification.

A remaining hairline at oblique angles came through the axial gap between the
raised black band and recessed gold. The black band now has a concealed rear
seat reaching X=2.45 mm, with an 0.08 mm radial flare at its rear edge. This
overlaps the gold inner wall even between polygon vertices. The five original
visible profile surfaces are preserved triangle-for-triangle, including both
front heights and the step. Gold, silver, glass, iris blades and URDFs remain
unchanged. `tools/seal_black_bezel_seat.py` checks a watertight mesh, fixed visible
surfaces, normals, full-circumference overlap and identical model copies. Both
visual and collision meshes are updated; immediate originals and hashes are in
`change-backups/sealed-black-bezel-seat-20261103/`.

Build 2.9.21 increases lens sag from 5 to 8 mm at the same 22.86 mm radius.
The rim stays at X=3.5 mm; the apex is now X=11.5 mm. Runtime geometry, exported
lens STLs and the reflection normal map use the same spherical-cap formula.
The reflectivity setting, surrounding rings, blades and control calibration are
unchanged. The immediately preceding 5 mm lens assets and source are preserved
under `change-backups/stronger-lens-dome-20261103/`.

Build 2.9.23 reduces the trial 8 mm dome to the requested 6 mm depth. The rim
remains at X=3.5 mm, with the apex now X=9.5 mm. Runtime geometry, normal maps
and both exported lens assets use this depth. The preceding 8 mm assets are
preserved in `change-backups/six-mm-lens-dome-20261103/`.
