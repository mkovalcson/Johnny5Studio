# Live Follow Me speed sliders

Press Left B → Follow speed sliders… while following. The main page contains All fingers + thumbs, Wrist roll, Wrist pitch, Arm twist, Elbow and Shoulder rotate, with each left/right pair sharing its slider. Head/body/travel and individual advanced finger sliders are available on secondary pages.

Speeds range from 0.1× to 5× of the original speed limit, take effect immediately and save automatically in Config/VR/FollowSpeeds.json. The original defaults remain 3× for shoulders/elbows, 2× for wrists/fingers and 1× for other controls, unless you have already saved different values. Existing saved speeds are retained.

The combined finger slider sets all finger and thumb joints to one value without changing wrist or arm speeds. Advanced finger settings remain available. See **VR-Follow-Arm-Hand-Speeds.md** for details, including mixed values and controls.
