# Present live through OBS or Teams

Stop timeline playback completely before opening Help.

## Present through OBS or Teams

1. Prepare webcam/controller mappings and click Meeting beside Record.
2. Choose **Use current editor setup** to inherit settings retained with Apply, or **Use saved scene** for a reusable complete setup. Check the robot, background and camera in the preview.
3. Turn on **Face tracking** and/or choose a **Controller**, then select the microphone. Speak to test the meter and mouth lights. Expand **Adjust mouth response** when needed.
4. Resolve any messages in **Ready to start** and choose **Start Meeting**, which also saves the settings. Capture the clean undocked URDF Model window in OBS and start OBS Virtual Camera. Connection instructions are available under **Connect to OBS / Teams…**.
5. In Teams select OBS Virtual Camera and your speech microphone separately.
6. Press Stop meeting when finished; the editor’s previous scene and camera are restored.

Expected result: live URDF performance without recording a take. The tracker owns the physical webcam, so Teams uses OBS. Meeting does not drive physical hardware.

For saving and export choices, see [Save, export and reuse your work](Saving.md).
